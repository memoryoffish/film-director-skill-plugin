# Hero (2002) – "Lake Fight": beat script + cutting

Source: `hero2002_lake_fight.mp4` (23.976 fps, 1280x720 with a 2.35:1 image at 1280x544, y=88).
The shot list is in `shots.json`. Times are frame-accurate (t0 = first frame of the shot). Short names used below:
**BLACK** = Swordsman in black, **BLUE** = Swordsman in blue, **WOMAN** = the dead woman on the bier.

How the scene works: it cuts between two places. In the **duel**, BLACK and BLUE fight. Part of it is a fast handheld sword fight on a grassy hillside, shot in real time; the rest is slow-motion flying over a mirror-still lake. In the **vigil**, BLUE stays beside WOMAN's body in a pavilion on the water. A water drop thrown up in the duel lands on her face like a tear, and that links the two places.

## Editing grammar (must keep)

1. **Two speeds that alternate.** The hillside fight is handheld, real-time, and grey-green, with shots of 0.9–2.2 s that often begin or end in motion blur or a whip pan. The lake shots are locked off and in slow motion, with blue-teal colour and shots of 1.5–8 s. Every few slow lake shots, a short burst of real-time fighting cuts in (shots 4–5, 11, 18, 23).
2. **Motif in pairs from two angles.** The sword tip at the waterline is always shown twice or more, alternating a wide angle with the pavilion and a tele angle without it. The lens is half above, half below the water. Each shot is about 1 s.
3. **Face-over-landscape blocks.** A wide lake shot is followed by a frontal, emotionless face CU dissolved over it at 60–70 % peak opacity for 4.5–6.5 s. Under the face, the base layer hard-cuts to a second lake wide. BLUE's face comes first, BLACK's second.
4. **An object carries the cut.** The water drop travels from the duel to the vigil: flung, tracked, deflected, returned, blocked, falls, lands. The camera follows the drop instead of the people, using tracking, tilts, and one match-dissolve.
5. **A triangle of close-ups.** BLUE CU/MCU, WOMAN ECU (always the same sideways head, with the drop on her cheek), and BLACK ECU (eyes behind an out-of-focus hilt). Each shot is 0.6–1.9 s and none of them move.
6. **Two-layer frames.** BLUE sits still in the foreground while BLACK flies in the background (shots 42, 54). The duel and the vigil share one frame.
7. **Screen direction.** On the hillside, BLUE is on the left facing right and BLACK is on the right. Over the water, they fly in from the right (37) or left (48).
8. **Wipes and blur as cuts.** Hair across the lens (35→36), a sleeve across the lens (43), a whip pan to blur (11→12, 36→37), and a whip-in from blur (18).
9. **Colour as structure.** Teal and blue throughout, with one red maple accent (15). The hillside is desaturated. The coda turns to white frost.
10. **The coda slows down.** After 104 s every shot is static and 2–4.6 s long, mostly frontal CUs and back views. The scene ends on a walk away and a man weeping, with no action.
