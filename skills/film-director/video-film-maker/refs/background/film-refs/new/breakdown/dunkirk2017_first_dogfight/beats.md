## Editing grammar to replicate

- **Each pilot has a fixed camera rig, so each face has its own look.**
  - Pilot 1 is shot low-frontal, centred, square to the lens, with sky and sea at the left window.
  - The Wingman is shot very low and wide from the panel looking up, with the canopy rail diagonal at left.
  - The Leader is shot off-centre left, with the dark canopy bar diagonal at right and latch hardware on it.
  - A second rig shows each pilot through the curved side glass from outside, with sun flare. This is the only place flare appears.
- **Look → see pairs.** Almost every POV is set up by a CU whose eyeline motivates it:
  - look up → enemy dive
  - look back → tail-cam or mirror
  - look right → gunsight
  - look left → wingman alongside

  Never cut to a POV without the motivating look first.
- **Face ↔ sight alternation in the attack.** From #33 to #44, cuts strictly alternate between Pilot 1's CU and the gunsight POV. CUs run 0.9–1.8 s and sight shots 0.3–2.8 s. The firing beat inserts an ECU of the thumb on the gun button (#37) and cuts on action into the sight.
- **The camera stays attached to an aircraft for the whole fight.** There are no air-to-air or aerial shots between #18 (86.2 s) and #60 (169.2 s): 83 s with the camera always inside or bolted to a plane. The "outside" view returns only to confirm the kill (#60) and for the regroup (#67).
- **Fixed compositions for the mounted cams, repeated identically:**
  - Tail-cam (#21, #27, #55): fin and fin-flash centre-right, tailplane reaching left, yellow-ringed roundel bottom-right. The enemy is a small silhouette upper-left or centre-left behind.
  - Cowling-cam (#32, #57): cowling and exhaust stack in the left third, wing root along the bottom, sea below. Targets sit in the right half and drift right and up.
  - Through-sight (#36–#53): faint reticle ring centred at about 70 % of frame height, with horizontal bars and a lower tick. The enemy enters from the right or upper-right and works toward the centre. The Wingman, when visible, is left of or above the enemy.
- **Screen direction.**
  - The flight travels screen-left → right (#0). Tail-cam looks aft, so the enemy is behind and "upper-left".
  - In the regroup, Pilot 1 looks screen-right (his left), the Wingman's fighter is in profile nose screen-right, and the Wingman looks screen-left back. This is a clean reverse eyeline.
- **The horizon is always the orientation anchor.** Banks are shown by tilted horizons (25–35° in the break and the chase, near-vertical in the #44 roll). Cockpit CUs keep a slice of sea and horizon in the side window.
- **Inserts mark decisions:** kneeboard (#2), gauge (#4), reticle on (#8), thumb fires (#37). Fuel and time is the ticking clock, and Pilot 1 glances at the gauge again in #64.
- **The enemy is faceless and mostly small.** It is large only in the 1 s overhead pass (#16) and in the crash (#60). Yellow nose and wingtips make it readable at 2 % of frame.
- **Grade:** overcast, desaturated teal-cyan sea and sky, warm amber skin, leather and shearling. White smoke and spray are the only bright accents.
